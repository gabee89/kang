# 지역 배경 제작 기록

도구: built-in image_gen (imagegen 스킬)

입력: 기존 terrain-v3.webp를 바탕으로 만든 지역 배경. 최종 요청에 맞춰 다섯 지역을 양쪽에 섞어 배치했습니다.

게임용 파일: assets/terrain-regions-v4.webp (생성 PNG를 WebP 품질 92로 변환, 그림 내용 변경 없음)

최종 프롬프트:

Edit this top-down game terrain. Keep the painterly soft 3D miniature forest style, camera, outside forest frame, and open winding-road corridors. The user wants all terrain types scattered on BOTH sides, not one large biome in each corner. Replace the five large separated zones with twelve smaller organically connected terrain patches, each with an open usable center, naturally blended into grass. Repeat grass groves, small ponds, ember clearings, thunder quartz glades and haunted ruins on both the left and right land islands. Placement centers in percentages of canvas (x,y): grass grove (28,27), haunted miniature ruined lantern glade (41,35), shallow pond (28,48), thunder quartz mossy glade (41,55), basalt ember clearing (28,70), grass grove (41,76); on the right island: shallow pond (62,27), basalt ember clearing (75,35), haunted miniature ruined lantern glade (62,48), grass grove (75,55), thunder quartz mossy glade (62,70), shallow pond (75,76). Patches have irregular edges and should not look like ordered colored tiles, boxes, circles, board squares or separated panels. Each patch spans around 12 to 16 percent canvas width and height, with natural transitions and gentle overlaps. Haunted patches have low stone ruins and tiny floating ghost flames, electric patches have pale quartz and tiny lightning veins; they must be visually distinct. No game characters, no text, no symbols, no UI, no placement markers, no pedestals. Keep clear road corridors x=17%,52%,83%, y=15%,85%. Interior assets must be low and unobtrusive, playable flat ground, no tall objects obscuring character placement. Preserve framing and aspect ratio. This is a coherent natural landscape with mixed small regions, not a biome chart.
